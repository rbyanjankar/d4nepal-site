Who we are

A small team that believes good analysis should not need a data team.

D4Nepal Inc. is a company registered in the State of Maryland, USA. We build data and analytics products that turn the numbers people already have into answers they can act on, for small businesses and for everyday life, including sport.

Our founder, Ramesh Byanjankar, comes from senior analytics and data leadership. He has spent a career turning messy data into decisions, and builds our products to put that same thinking in the hands of people who never had an analyst to ask.

Giving back

Preparing for the future.

Alongside our products, D4Nepal gives high school and college students real work experience on real products, as our way of giving back to the community. It is a fun, creative way to gather, analyze and visually organize ideas and information into a simple, dynamic solution.

Why we exist

Big companies have analysts. Everyone else has spreadsheets.

People track more than ever, from sales and bookings to miles and results, and are still left guessing what it all means. We think that gap is worth closing, and we hold ourselves to three rules while we do it.

Plain English first

Every number comes with a sentence a busy person can act on. No jargon to decode.

Honest about what data can say

We describe patterns, not proof of cause. When we do not know, we say so. We are not a financial, medical or legal advisor.

Your data is yours

Each person and business sees only their own data, and you can delete your account and everything in it, with a 30-day window to change your mind.
