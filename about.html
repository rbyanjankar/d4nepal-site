The roadmap: five pillars of a successful solution

Every solution rests on the same five things. Each one gets real practice.

Stakeholders

Identify the stakeholders and sponsors, and learn why you need them. Hold 1:1 meetings and build partnership and collaboration. They are the most important of all: they provide the budget and the requirements, and they give the final sign-off.

Analysis

Is the project feasible, and what is the return? Are there any gaps? Is there enough data and information to answer every question? Define the KPIs and measures that lead to actionable insights.

Learning agenda

A systematic, organized way of answering questions, with a tentative look and feel of the solution, the wireframe. It checks that all the information and logistics are available and that nothing critical is missing.

Visuals

Design the solution in Excel first, then build it in visualization tools such as Tableau and Power BI, and share the finished work in the tool communities.

Communications

Be a storyteller. Know your audience, communicate the why with facts, and design effective slides. Keep stakeholders and end users updated, and practise the presentation or sales pitch.
